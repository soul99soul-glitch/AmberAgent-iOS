Amber changes the address plan, restricts routing and packet reflection to
one local peer, validates packet lengths, and serializes provider lifecycle
callbacks. LocalPacketReflection.swift is Amber's implementation of the
address swap; Jitterbug's packetReplaceIp helper is not included.

LocalDevVPN was also inspected to understand the address plan already proven
on the test device. Its provider implementation is not copied into this
extension. This is source-informed integration, not a clean-room claim.
